Privacy Policy — Halo: Floating Button

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Halo: Floating Button ("Halo", "the app") is a personal utility that shows a floating on-screen button for quick actions such as volume, torch, screenshot, screen lock and launching apps. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

In short: Halo does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you configure stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Halo does not collect or transmit any personal or identifying information. The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (button colour, size, transparency, chosen actions, position and preferences) are saved only in the app's private storage on your own device. This information never leaves your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Network use

Halo makes one type of network request: an update check. The app asks GitHub's public releases service whether a newer version of Halo is available. This request contains no personal data from the app. Standard technical information (such as your device's IP address) is handled by GitHub under GitHub's own privacy policy. Halo does not send any other data anywhere.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Display over other apps — to show the floating button on top of other screens.
  • Accessibility service — used only to lock the screen or take a screenshot when you tap those buttons. Halo does not read, record, or collect screen content, text, or what you do in other apps.
  • Camera (flashlight) — used only to turn the torch on and off. Halo never opens the camera for photos or video.
  • Notifications — to show the small ongoing notification Android requires while the button is running.
  • Run at startup — to bring the button back after you restart your phone, if you enable that option.

Children's privacy

Halo is a general-purpose utility, collects no data, and is not directed at children.
